Keep Bodoni Moda for the masthead only. Its hairline strokes look great at display sizes but break up in small headlines and in dark mode.
Newsreader was designed for reading news on screen. Its optical sizes (6–72) keep one family crisp from captions up to headlines, and serif body text marks “this is the article.”
Instrument Sans handles interface text. It is narrower and sharper than Lato, so dense controls fit more easily.
JetBrains Mono sets addresses and content IDs. Signatures are part of the content here, so they deserve their own typeface.
